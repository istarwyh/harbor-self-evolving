An Agent can rewrite its prompt, tools or Evaluator and still become worse. “It changed” is not “it improved.”

A trustworthy loop fixes the business cases, execution identity and scoring contract before the change. It separates the Optimizer that proposes a change from the Gate that judges promotion.
