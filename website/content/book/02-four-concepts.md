Start with four user-facing concepts: cases, execution, judgment and controlled change. Harbor can compile internal roles after the business contract is clear.

## You do {#you-do}

Choose representative cases, define what the Agent produces, write criteria with evidence requirements, and constrain what may change.

## Harbor records {#harbor-records}

Dataset and Stack manifests, criterion identities, Candidate binding and execution environment.

## This does not prove {#does-not-prove}

A valid manifest proves structure and identity—not that the cases represent production or the Evaluator is correct.
